# Guide to Using OpenAir

Follow these steps to log in to OpenAir, manage timesheets, and track your work hours effectively. OpenAir is used by our team for time tracking, maintaining projects, and generating time and activity reports.

---

### Logging In to OpenAir

1.  **Access OpenAir:** Log in to your OpenAir account using Advantive as the **CompanyID**.
2.  **View Dashboard:** Once logged in, you’ll see your dashboard, which displays your timesheets and other relevant information.

![](https://alinasergeeva6.github.io/pepepri-docs/static/image.png)

---

### Opening an Existing Timesheet

1.  **Locate Timesheet:** In the **My Status** panel on the dashboard, click **Open Timesheet**.

![](https://alinasergeeva6.github.io/pepepri-docs/static/image-1.png)

1.  **Select Timesheet:** A list of your opened timesheets will appear. Choose the one you need and click on it to open.

![](https://alinasergeeva6.github.io/pepepri-docs/static/image-2.png)

---

### Creating a New Timesheet

1.  **Navigate to Timesheet Section:** In the sidebar, click **Timesheet**, then select **Open**.

![](https://alinasergeeva6.github.io/pepepri-docs/static/image-3.png)

1.  **Create New Timesheet:** Click **Create** > **New**.

![](https://alinasergeeva6.github.io/pepepri-docs/static/image-4.png)

1.  **Set Start Date:** In the opened menu, select the starting date for the timesheet and click **Save**.

---

### Filling Out a Timesheet

Once you’ve opened an existing timesheet or created a new one, follow these steps to fill it out:

1.  **Select Project and Task:**
    1.  In the **Project** column, choose the project you were assigned to and worked on.
    2.  In the **Task** column, select the task group related to the project. Refer to the table below for task group numbers:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ***Billable*** | *Select assigned* ***Project*** |
| ***Non-Billable Internal*** *group* | *Select project* ***IC Advantive, LLC: Internal Administrative*** |
| **Support** | 22: Support to Customer Support |
| **Presale** | 21: Support to Internal Sales |
| **Internal Meetings** | 31: Internal Meetings |
| **Internal add-ons/articles** | 25: Support To Product Development |
| **Training (for both Trainer and Trainee)** | 13: PMI Training |
| **General Correspondence Emails** | 33: Administrative - timekeeping |
| **Review Current To-Do List + Timelogs Tracking** | 33: Administrative - timekeeping |
| ***Days Off/Holidays/Sick Leave*** | *Select project* ***IC\_Advantive, LLC : Time Off*** |
| **Paid Time Off** | 1: Time Off - Paid |
| **National Holidays** | 5: Public/Bank Holiday |
| **Sick Leave** | 1: Time Off - Paid |

1.  **Set Time Type:** In the **Time Type** column, select **Remote**.
2.  **Fill in Hours:**
    -   The timesheet grid is divided by days, starting from Monday (the second column).
    -   Enter the amount of time spent on the task in the appropriate day’s cell.
    -   OpenAir uses the following format for time:
        -   1 = 1 hour
        -   1.5 = 1 hour and 30 minutes
        -   1.75 = 1 hour and 45 minutes, etc.

3.  **Add Comments:**
    -   In the comment section, always describe what you did for the task.
    -   Use the format: \[Task Name\] - \[What you did in the task\].

4.  **Save Changes:** After filling out the timesheet, click **Save** to ensure your changes are applied.

---

### Submitting Your Timesheet

-   **Weekly Submission:** Every Friday, ensure your timesheet is filled out for all days of the week. Once complete, click **Submit** by 16:30 to meet the deadline.

---

### Important Notes

-   **Maximum Hours:** The maximum time you can track per day in Smartsheet is **8 hours**. Ensure your OpenAir entries align with this limit.
-   **Accuracy:** Double-check your project, task, and hours to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
