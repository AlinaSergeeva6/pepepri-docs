# Inviting External Guests to Meetings in Microsoft Teams

Overview

Microsoft Teams enables users to schedule and host meetings with both internal and external participants. This guide focuses on the process of inviting external guests, from scheduling the meeting to managing their entry into the meeting room, ensuring a smooth experience for all attendees.

***

### Steps to Invite External Guests

#### Step 1: Schedule a Meeting

1. Open the Microsoft Teams client.
2. Navigate to the **Meetings** tab on the left sidebar.
3.  Click the **Schedule a Meeting** button.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (610).png" alt="" width="297"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4. Fill in the meeting details:
   * **Title:** Provide a descriptive name for the meeting.
   * **Date:** Select the date and time for the meeting.
   * **Location:** Specify if it’s a virtual meeting or include a physical location if applicable.<br>

#### Step 2: Invite an External Guest

1. In the meeting creation window, locate the **Invite People** bar.
2. Enter the email address of the external guest.
3.  Once the email is entered, a username suggestion will pop up. Click the suggestion to add the guest to the invitee list.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (612).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 3: Finalize and Schedule

1. Review the meeting details for accuracy.
2. Click the **Schedule** button to create the meeting.<br>
3. A summary of the meeting will appear, confirming the creation.<br>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (613).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 4: Guest Receives Invitation

* The external guest will receive an email invitation containing a **Join Microsoft Teams Meeting** link.
*   The email will include meeting details such as the title, date, and time.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (615).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 5: Guest Joins the Meeting

1. The guest clicks the **Join Microsoft Teams Meeting** link in the email.
2. The guest will be prompted to:
   * Configure their **Display Name**.
   *   Set up their audio devices (e.g., microphone, speakers).<br>

       <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (616).png" alt="" width="448"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. After configuration, the guest clicks **Join Now** and is directed to the meeting lobby.
4. The meeting will not start until the organizer joins.

#### Step 6: Organizer Manages the Meeting

1.  The meeting organizer receives a pop-up notification to join the meeting.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (617).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2. Upon joining, the organizer can view participants in the lobby.
3. The organizer invites the guest from the lobby into the meeting room to start the session.

***

### Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during this process (e.g., the guest cannot join, or the invitation email is not received), contact your IT team for assistance.
