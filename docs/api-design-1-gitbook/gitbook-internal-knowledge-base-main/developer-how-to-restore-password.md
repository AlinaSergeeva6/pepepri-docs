# Developer - How to restore password

On the login page, click on **Lost your password** link:

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (1091).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Pic. 1</p></figcaption></figure>

Enter your email address and click on **Get New Password**:

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (1092).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Pic. 2</p></figcaption></figure>

After email validation you’ll get this message:

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (1093).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Pic. 3</p></figcaption></figure>

Check your email and follow the link to reset your password.
