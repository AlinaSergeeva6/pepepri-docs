# Pepperi Trade Promotions

*Pepperi Trade Promotions Overview*

Setting up promotions is a key component of any B2B sales strategy, enabling you to upsell and cross-sell to existing customers while attracting new ones. Pepperi offers a flexible and user-friendly platform to create and distribute promotions across various sales channels, tailored to different user types.

---

### Overview

Pepperi’s Trade Promotions feature allows you to design a variety of promotions using a graphical promotions editor, accessible to non-technical users. These promotions can be pushed to:

-   Field sales reps
-   B2B eCommerce buyers
-   Telesales teams

For example, in-person sales reps might receive a discount on items or special package offers, while B2B buyers can access different promotion types, all configurable within the same intuitive interface.

#### Key Features

-   **Customizable Scope:** Apply promotions to items, item categories, brands, or any other property.
-   **Targeted Application:** Easily assign promotions to specific accounts while excluding others.
-   **User-Specific Access:** Managers and users can focus on their own brands and promotions, with an option to restrict visibility to only the promotions they create.

---

### Promotion Types

Promotions are categorized into discounts and incentives applied to **Items**, **Packages/Bundles**, and **Orders**. Each type supports flexible configurations with quantity tiers, date ranges, and property-based filtering.

#### Promotion Priority

-   Promotions can have assigned priorities to manage eligibility when an item or customer qualifies for multiple promotions.
-   Example: If an item qualifies for a "Buy 10 get 5% discount" and a 20% brand discount, you can specify whether both or only one applies.
-   **Flexibility:**
    -   Limit promotions to specific date ranges or keep them active indefinitely.
    -   Filter by properties such as customer segments, brands, or order details.

#### Examples of Property-Based Promotions

-   Discount on a specific brand for a customer segment (e.g., filtered by account size or location).
-   $10 special price on seasonal items.
-   3% order discount for self-service B2B Storefront orders.
-   70% off on last season’s clearance items.

The Promotions Editor includes a filter to quickly identify applicable promotions based on their properties.

---

### Item Promotions

Incentives are applied individually to items based on quantities or amounts purchased.

#### Quantity-Based Incentives

-   **Structure:** Buy "X or more" of an item to receive:
    -   Discount %
    -   Discounted Price
    -   Total Price
    -   Additional item(s) free (same or different)
    -   Additional item(s) discounted (same or different)

-   **Multi-Tier Support:** Set multiple quantity tiers with unique or consistent incentives.

#### Amount-Based Incentives

-   **Structure:** Buy "$X or more" of an item to receive:
    -   $Y off
    -   Z% discount

#### Examples

-   $3.00 off on specific items/brands (filtered by property or category).
-   3% discount on specific items/brands.
-   Buy 20 or more: 10% discount; 50: 20%; 100: 30%; 200: 50%.
-   Buy 15 or more: Pay $30; 50: Get 5 free; 80: 30% off.
-   Buy 1, get the second at 50%.
-   Buy 5, get 1 free (same or different item).

---

### Packages and Bundles

#### Packages

-   **Definition:** Incentives on a group of items selected by the customer from a predefined list.
-   **Example:** "Pick any 5 items from the list for $25" with quantity tiers (e.g., 15 items for $60, 25 items for $75).
-   **Limitations:** Restrict usage (e.g., "Buy 5 for $25, limited to the first 10 purchases").

#### Bundles

-   **Definition:** Incentives on predefined groups of items sold together.
-   **Example:** A bundle of 12 Item1, 24 Item2, and 18 Item3 for a fixed price of $375.
-   **Limitations:** Cap the number of bundles per order or allow unlimited purchases.
-   **Additional Incentives:** Offer free in-store displays for purchasing multiple bundles (e.g., buy 4 bundles, get 1 free display).

#### Examples

-   Select 50 items from a list for $200.
-   Select 25 items from specific brands, then get 1 free (or discounted) item from another brand.
-   Select 4 items, get the cheapest free.
-   Buy $200 worth of a category, get $25 worth of items free from another category.

---

### Order Promotions

Incentives are applied based on the total order amount, payment method, or other order properties.

#### Structure

-   Buy "$X or more" to receive:
    -   Discount %
    -   Discounted Price
    -   Additional item(s) free (predefined or user-selected)
    -   Additional item(s) discounted (predefined or user-selected)

#### Examples

-   Buy $500 or more: 5% discount; $1000: 6%; $2000: 7%.
-   Buy $750 or more: Choose 3 free items from a list (via an additional cart screen).
-   Pay with cash: Get 3% discount (independent of order total).
