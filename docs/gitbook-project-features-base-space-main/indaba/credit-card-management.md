# Credit Card Management

Activity that lets users view, remove and tokenize credit cards of the selected account. Displayed information is received from NetSuite and stored inside the UDT.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (32).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Credit Card Management</p></figcaption></figure>

In order to tokenize a new card, the user can click "Add New" and get redirected to another UI task to enter card info. Tokenization of new cards is performed by NetSuite Payment Instruments.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (33).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Adding new credit card</p></figcaption></figure>
